# Commodity side — CTA and CPO, quoted, and where publishing sits

**7 U.S.C. §1a** is the definitions section. Re-fetch before citing; the carve-outs are conditional and the
conditions matter more than the headline.

## Commodity trading advisor — §1a(12)(A)

> *"any person who — (i) **for compensation or profit, engages in the business of advising others**, either
> directly or through **publications, writings, or electronic media**, as to the value of or the advisability
> of trading in [commodity interests] …"*
>
> *"(ii) for compensation or profit, and as part of a regular business, **issues or promulgates analyses or
> reports** concerning any of the activities referred to in clause (i)"*

**Note the asymmetry with the securities statute:** "electronic media" is named explicitly, and (ii) reaches
**analyses or reports** — not only advice.

## The publisher carve-out — §1a(12)(B)

Excluded from "commodity trading advisor", *subject to subparagraph (C)*:

> *"(ii) any **news reporter, news columnist, or news editor** of the print or electronic media, or any lawyer,
> accountant, or teacher;"*
>
> *"(iv) **the publisher or producer of any print or electronic data of general and regular dissemination**,
> including its employees;"*

**Two cautions.** (1) This is *not* the securities wording — do not carry that case law across. (2)
**Subparagraph (C) conditions these exclusions and is easy to skip; read it before relying on (iv).**

## Commodity pool operator — §1a(11)(A)(i)

> *"any person — (i) engaged in a business that is of the nature of a commodity pool … and who, in connection
> therewith, **solicits, accepts, or receives from others, funds, securities, or property** … for the purpose of
> trading in commodity interests"*

**This is the "other people's money" prong.** No funds taken, no pool.

## The decision table

| situation | answer | basis |
|---|---|---|
| We publish what we did with **our own** money | **not a CTA on these facts** — the prong needs advising *others* | (12)(A)(i) |
| We **sell** trading analysis or calls on commodity interests | **real CTA risk** — (12)(A)(ii) is expressly engaged by issuing analyses for compensation | (12)(A)(ii) + (B)(iv) |
| We accept funds to trade for someone else | **CPO territory** | (11)(A)(i) |
| We display market-implied probabilities as data | not advising; **describing is not directing** | (12)(A)(i) |
| We tell people **how to trade** those contracts | crosses into advising, and ad platforms prohibit signal framing outright | (12)(A)(ii) + platform policy |

The line in one sentence: **the derivatives regime cares when you advise others or hold their money — not
when you publish what you did with your own.** Monetising the analysis is the crossing, and it is a decision,
not an accident.
