# Anthropic Fellows Program — AI Safety Research (2026 cohorts)

## Overview

Anthropic pays engineers and researchers to work on its highest-priority AI safety research questions, with
Anthropic researcher mentorship and a compute budget. 200+ fellows since early 2025; **65%+ of fellows have
published work** from their fellowship. **35%+ of past fellows were hired by Anthropic full-time**, and many
more went full-time on safety elsewhere.

**Source:** https://x.com/suraj_sharma14/status/2110377588975943987 (shared by Jordan 2026-10-14)

## What They're Looking For

Their words: *"We care much more about your ability to execute on research than your credentials."*
You **don't need a PhD, prior ML experience, or published papers** — past fellows came from physics,
mathematics, computer science, cybersecurity and other quantitative backgrounds.

- Technical fundamentals — code well, use AI tools like Claude, take ambiguous problems and make concrete progress
- Motivation — genuinely wants to reduce catastrophic risk from advanced AI
- Ability to learn and ship — picks up skills fast, debugs when things break, drives forward under uncertainty

## The Jordan Hook (why he is not an outsider here)

The program's **AI security** track already contains a fellow project that used autonomous agents to
**identify $4.6M in blockchain smart contract vulnerabilities** and discover two novel zero-days
(red.anthropic.com/2025/smart-contracts/). That is Jordan's exact intersection — he runs an autonomous agent
fleet against on-chain systems with real keys and real rails: x402 payment gateways, LP position readers, wallet
custody, three chains. Most applicants can claim either agents or crypto; very few have a **live fleet doing
custody-bound on-chain work**, which is the thing this track demonstrably values.

Portfolio-first framing: GenTech Labs (agent fleet + x402 + on-chain execution) *is* the evidence of "ability to
execute on ambiguous technical problems" — and the failure/forensics work (key loss, custody design, verification
layers) is directly adjacent to AI security and AI control.

## Verdict

Apply by **2026-10-18**, and state the Amazon commitment as a constraint in the application — their own wording
invites it and promises case-by-case review. Remote solves *where*, not *how much*: running both is ~40 fellowship
hours + ~34 Amazon hours, and the published paper is the deliverable that suffers first if the load wins. The
decision point is the offer, not the application.
